## The joke

"I put 'Do Not Disturb' on my calendar, so my boss booked a meeting
called 'Quick Sync About Your Do Not Disturb'."

Why this line, against the published judge brief (most important first):
1. One-line joke about office life — yes, a single sentence, a joke.
2. Funny — the setup (Do Not Disturb) is the one shield that is supposed
   to stop meetings; the payoff defeats it with a meeting about the
   shield itself. Surprising and recognisable to anyone in an office.
3. One line — a single sentence, no rambling.
4. Fresh — a specific detail (the quoted meeting title) rather than a
   generic "too many meetings" complaint; not a joke everyone has heard.
5. Clear on first read — tight wording, no reference needing explanation.

Pattern: same shape as the ai-joke-03 v3 winner ("Never touch the sprint
board" — a rule, then someone breaks the rule, then restores it): a rule
meant to stop something, broken by the very thing it was meant to stop.
